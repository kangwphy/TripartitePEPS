\section{State preparation is not boundary VUMPS}
For RK--Ising,
\[
 |\Psi_\beta\rangle=Z^{-1/2}\sum_s
 e^{\frac\beta2\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}s_i s_j}|s\rangle,
 \qquad s_i=\pm1.
\]
Factor $e^{\beta st/2}=\sum_uQ_{su}Q_{tu}$ and set
$A^s_{uldr}=Q_{su}Q_{sl}Q_{sd}Q_{sr}$. This is an exact $D=2$ PEPS, not a
variationally optimized state. Its norm is the classical Ising partition
function, providing an absolute free-energy check. The half in the Boltzmann
exponent is elementwise, not a matrix square root of the full Boltzmann matrix.

For the square-lattice TFIM, with Pauli matrices,
$H=-\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}\sigma_i^z\sigma_j^z-h\sum_i\sigma_i^x$.
Current production minimizes the PEPS energy with AD through CTMRG, initialized
from random tensors, simple update, or saved continuation states. Simple update
is an initializer, not a variational convergence certificate. Retain both
continuation branches, energies, projected gradients and source-state hashes;
recheck selected tensors with fresh environments.

\textbf{VUMPS subsequently solves the norm-transfer boundary of the saved
PEPS; it is not the current TFIM PEPS optimizer.} Distinguish PEPS dimension
$D$, optimization environment dimension $\chi_{\rm CTM}$, and measurement
boundary dimension $\chi$. Check spatial symmetry on the actual tensor and
transfer operator before reusing regional boundaries. A symmetric Hamiltonian
alone is insufficient; spatial C4v, internal Ising symmetry and MPS gauge
freedom are different notions.

\section{Boundary MPS and local growth}
Contract the physical spin between PEPS ket and bra to obtain one local norm
tensor $a_{nwse}$. Its fused ket/bra virtual legs have dimension $D^2$; retain
their ordering for replicas. $T$ denotes the entire repeated transfer row or
column, never one $a$. VUMPS approximates its dominant boundary using
\[
 A_C^p=A_L^pC=CA_R^p,\quad
 \sum_p(A_L^p)^\dagger A_L^p=I,\quad
 \sum_p A_R^p(A_R^p)^\dagger=I.
\]
These identities concern specified self-overlap channels, not arbitrary mixed
spatial overlaps. Check Galerkin, environment and center residuals. Record
competing low-residual candidates and the selection rule near degeneracy.

One local absorption is
\[
 (\Dg^p)_{(\alpha,w),(\beta,e)}
 =\sum_n(A_L^n)_{\alpha\beta}a_{nwpe}.
\]
This creates a bond of dimension $\chi D^2$. It contains one $a$; an entire
grown tail contains one $a$ at every layer.

\section{Three distinct contraction routes}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Bare ladders.} Contract the full $M_L|M_R$ ladder to obtain
$G_{\rm bare}$ and the full $M_L|a|M_R$ ladder to obtain $B_{\rm ladder}[p]$.
Their open spaces differ. Sector, scale and support compatibility must be
established before treating $B_{\rm ladder}G_{\rm bare}^{+}$ as the desired LMPS.
Orient the columns first: in the current north-facing quantum adapter, raw
west bonds run south-to-north and raw south bonds east-to-west. Exchange
their left/right axes for a top-to-bottom ladder and a left-to-right reference
strip, respectively; this is not complex conjugation or a gauge fit.
\item \textbf{Direct grown-tail: current TFIM production label.} Independently iterate
\[
 L_{n+1}=\sum_p(A_L^p)^\dagger L_n\Dg^p,\qquad
 R_{n+1}=\sum_p\Dg^pR_n(A_R^p)^\dagger.
\]
Form $G_{\rm grow}=LR$, $B^p=L\Dg^pR$, and a transfer-normalized
$H^p\propto B^pG_{\rm grow}^+$. No endpoint checkpoint is imported. This
does \emph{not} establish $G_{\rm bare}=G_{\rm grow}$.
\item \textbf{VUMPS endpoint.} Obtain the left/right environments and place the
center matrix in compatible enlarged-bond coordinates. Form
$O_V=L_{\rm end}R_{\rm end}$ and
$B_{\rm end}^p=L_{\rm end}\Dg^pR_{\rm end}$; test
$B_{\rm end}^p\simeq\kappa A_L^pO_V$. These environments are not arbitrary
density-projector eigenvectors simply renamed $V_L^\dagger,V_R$.
\end{enumerate}

No unconditional identity $O_V=G_{\rm bare}$ is assumed. If a replacement
inserts $Q=R(LR)^{-1}L$ at an original network cut, its error is
$Z-Z_Q=E_{\rm left}(I-Q)E_{\rm right}$; $Q^2=Q$ does not make this zero.
Ill-conditioned inverses amplify residuals, so report singular values and
retained support. Direct/endmap agreement tests those implementations on
those states; it is not a general factorization theorem or a test of all
shared downstream code.

\section{LMPS geometry and six-$R$ contraction}
Regions $A,B$ are the upper quadrants and $C$ is the lower half-plane. Build
oriented horizontal and vertical rails, transporting every incident retained
bond consistently. Their shared-chart seam pairs are
$AB:(v_L,v_R)$, $AC:(h_L,h_L)$, $BC:(h_R,h_R)$; independent regions carry
their own rail tensors. A vertical rail obtained by reshaping an environment
is not simply the original $A_R$.

Solve one-copy mixed seam maps $K_{AB},K_{AC},K_{BC}$ with physical far caps.
Do not replace all of them by identity solely because C4v is available.
For four replicas, region $X$ pairs ket $i$ with bra $\sigma_X(i)$, with
\[
 \sigma_A=\mathrm{id},\qquad\sigma_B=(12)(34),\qquad\sigma_C=(13)(24).
\]
The relative two-cycles produce six rank-four endpoints
$AB12,AB34,AC13,AC24,BC14,BC23$. Their twelve shared regional/copy legs
close $Z_4$ on an octahedron. The ordinary two-replica sectors reuse them:
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lll}\toprule
Sector & Rank-four endpoints & Untwisted seam\\\midrule
$Z_{2A}$ & $AB12,AC13$ & $BC$\\
$Z_{2B}$ & $AB34,BC14$ & $AC$\\
$Z_{2C}$ & $AC24,BC23$ & $AB$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
The untwisted $Z_1$ uses the three one-copy maps. A $Z_{2X}$ is not a bare
trace of two rank-four tensors. Check left/right eigen-residuals and that the
physical far cap converges to the chosen endpoint; retain its asymptotic
amplitude, not just a unit-norm eigenvector.

With synchronized normalization,
\[
 S_2(X)=-\ln\frac{Z_{2X}}{Z_1^2},\quad
 S_2^{(3)}=-\frac12\ln\frac{Z_4}{Z_1^4},\quad
 \boxed{\tS=-\ln\frac{Z_4Z_1^2}{Z_{2A}Z_{2B}Z_{2C}}}.
\]
Remove infinite-seam growth consistently. Independent rescalings of every
endpoint and one-copy map must cancel in $\tS$. Production components may
be straight-reference-subtracted constants and need not be positive entropies.
``S3'' denotes the tripartite quantity, not ordinary R\'enyi order three.

\section{Convergence and reporting}
Use $\xi=-\ell/\ln|\lambda_1/\lambda_0|$, specifying the channel, eigenvalue
sector and transfer step length $\ell$. Boundary self-transfer, CTMRG transfer
and physical connected-spin lengths need not coincide at finite truncation;
there is no universal factor-of-two correction for broken boundary states.

Fix the saved PEPS while scanning $\chi$. Plot three rows: $\xi(\chi)$,
$\tS(\chi)$ and $\tS$ against the same run's $\xi$. An optimized finite-$D$
TFIM state can have a saturated correlation length near the physical transition,
unlike an exactly critical RK tensor. Bracket a finite-$D$ pseudocritical
region using dense $h$ scans, branch energies, magnetizations and $\xi(h)$
at increasing environment accuracy. A largest sampled $\xi$ is not an exact
critical field. Fit logarithms only in a demonstrated window with systematic
uncertainties.

Pin source hashes and retain components, seeds, tolerances, condition numbers
and checkpoints. Individual convergence and paired agreement are separate
tests; keep failures and missing points visible. All numerical tests and
plotting run through Slurm.
